What changed in Tax Year 2026-27

New section numbers

The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026. Salary TDS moves to Section 392, other payments to 393 (resident 393(1), non-resident 393(2), winnings, cash withdrawal and partner payments 393(3)), and TCS to 394.

Codes, forms and challans

Returns use numeric payment codes such as 1023 and 1027. Forms are renumbered (26Q becomes 140, 24Q becomes 138, 27EQ becomes 143), and new challans must be used for Tax Year 2026-27.

Updated thresholds

Commission or brokerage (194H) is 2% above ₹20,000. Professional services (194J) split into 2% and 10% buckets with a ₹50,000 limit. Rent has a ₹50,000 per month limit. Partner payments (194T) are taxed at 10% above ₹20,000.

How the calculator decides

  1. Payee type from PAN. The 4th letter sets the rate where it differs, such as contractors (1% or 2%) and insurance commission.
  2. Threshold check. Single-payment, monthly and yearly limits are tested separately. When a yearly limit is crossed, tax is also caught up on earlier payments.
  3. GST base. If GST is shown separately on the invoice, TDS is taken on the value before GST. GST is still paid to the vendor.
  4. Net payable. Invoice total minus TDS, retention, advances and other deductions, with the deposit due date.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using old section references in accounting software. Update masters to 392, 393 and 394 and the new codes.
  • Ignoring thresholds, or testing a yearly limit on a single bill.
  • Picking the wrong payment code. A wrong nature can bring a notice even when the tax amount is right.
  • Accepting a missing or inoperative PAN. The higher rate applies, at least 20% (5% for 194Q and 194O).
  • Forgetting DTAA, surcharge and cess on non-resident payments.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tax Year 2026-27 the first year under the new Act?

Yes. The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026, so old section numbers are useful for recognition only.

What are codes 1023, 1024 and 1027?

1023 is contractor payments to an Individual or HUF (1%). 1024 is contractors that are others (2%). 1027 is professional fees, the old 194J(b), at 10%.

Is TDS deducted on the GST amount?

Not when GST is shown separately on the invoice. TDS is taken on the value before GST, and GST is paid to the vendor in full.

Can I deduct TDS below the threshold?

Yes, you can deduct voluntarily using the calculator’s voluntary mode. It must be deposited and reported in the return, and the payee receives credit in Form 26AS.

What if the payee has no PAN or an inoperative PAN?

TDS is deducted at the higher of twice the rate or 20%. For 194Q and 194O it is 5%.

Why do some figures differ between sources?

The Income Tax Department page, GimBooks and GoForFiling differ on a few items, such as insurance commission (2% or 5%) and e-commerce (0.1% or 1%). This site uses 2% and 0.1%. Always confirm with the official notification.
