# Form Filler · free PDF form filler for your phone

Fill, tick, date, sign and add your photo to any PDF form on your phone, then save it or send it. No printer, no pen, no scanning.

**Try it:** https://rajmans123-netizen.github.io/form-filler/

- **Tap a box and type.** Letter boxes get one capital letter each (dates jump into DD / MM / YYYY groups). Lines and table cells line up by themselves.
- **Tick, date, sign, photo.** Draw your signature with a finger, or snap the one you signed on paper and the paper is removed. Photos are cropped to the form's photo box.
- **Save your details once** (name, date of birth, mobile, address) and put them into any form with one tap.
- **Save or send.** Shrink it under a KB limit, then send it on WhatsApp or email from the share sheet.
- **Private:** nothing is uploaded. Your form, details and signature stay in your phone's browser.

**How it works:** pages are drawn with [pdf.js](https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/). When you tap, `detect.js` reads the printed lines around your finger and decides whether it's a row of letter boxes, a box or table cell, or a line to write on. What you add is written into the original PDF with [pdf-lib](https://pdf-lib.js.org/), so the form's own text stays sharp. A PDF with a password, a photo of a paper form, or a size limit is saved as pictures of the pages.

**Honest limits:** very faint or hand-drawn boxes may need a nudge; Hindi and other scripts are saved as a picture of the text; a photo of a paper form works best taken straight on.

The sample form is made up for trying the app. It isn't an official form.
